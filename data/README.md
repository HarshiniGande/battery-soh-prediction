# Data

The four `.mat` files are the NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE) Li-ion battery aging dataset:

> B. Saha and K. Goebel (2007). "Battery Data Set", NASA Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA.

The dataset is publicly available from the NASA Prognostics Data Repository and is also mirrored on Kaggle and GitHub.

**Experiment summary (from the dataset documentation):** 18650 Li-ion cells were run through charge, discharge, and impedance cycles at room temperature (24 C).

* Charge: constant current 1.5 A until 4.2 V, then constant voltage until current drops to 20 mA.
* Discharge: constant current 2 A until the voltage falls to 2.7 V (B0005), 2.5 V (B0006, B0018), or 2.2 V (B0007).
* End of life: 30% capacity fade, from 2.0 Ah rated to 1.4 Ah.

`processed/cycle_features.csv` is generated by `run_pipeline.py` (one row per discharge cycle with engineered features and capacity).
Run `python run_pipeline.py --save-timeseries` to also export the 2-million-row long time-series table (about 60 MB, not committed).
